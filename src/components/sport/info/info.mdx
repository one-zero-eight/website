## Sport hours

### How many hours should I get per semester?

You should get 30 hours.

### How can I get sport hours throughout a semester?

Firstly, choose a sport or sport club for the whole semester.

Secondly, try to train at least once per week on schedule.

Additional opportunities to get extra hours:

1. Use self-sport options and upload results on the site.
2. Participate in sports events.

### What are online students supposed to do to pass the sport course?

The same as for students with the Special 2 health group.

### I have a medical reference about an illness. What do I need to do?

Upload this reference on the site. After approval, you'll get 2 sport hours per week depending on the time frame.

### I have an injury or illness. What should I do?

Take a medical reference and upload it to the site.

### How can I change the chosen sport?

The selected sport will be reset automatically at the beginning of the new semester.

### I'm an online student. Why do I need to pass online if I got 30 hours?

The exam consists of 2 parts:

1. Get 30 hours.
2. Pass the fitness test.

You can't pass the fitness test, so you're defending the project online.

Online students can pass by doing only the project.

## Fitness test

### I'm from Special 2 group, how can I pass the fitness test?

You will defend a project. It will be a presentation for 10 minutes and 5 minutes for questions. Check out your email box. There will be instructions.

### I'm from Special 1 health group. Do I need to pass the fitness test?

Yes, you are. But without any standards. In other words, you need to participate in this event and do your best according to your health and abilities, as much as you think it won't hurt you.

### When will the retake be?

First retake will be 24.09.2022.

Second retake will be 26.11.2022.

## Medical check-up

### Why do I need to undergo a medical check-up?

To be able to get hours, and not just visit a sports complex.

### What kind of certificate do I need to get? Will the help on the form "086u" be suitable?

You need to get a certificate about the health group for physical education classes.

If the therapist indicates a health group in the reference "086u", then this will also work.

### What should I do to get a medical check-up in Innopolis?

1. If you are a student from another region of Russia, you need to change your OMS medical insurance to a local one.
2. Go to the reception at the medical center and say that you are a student and want to pass a medical check-up to get a health group.
3. You will need to pass tests and bring a fluorography (optional) in printed form.

### How can I change my OMS medical insurance to a local one?

1. At the beginning of the semester, we invite insurance agents to do this.
2. If you couldn't change it with our help, find any insurance point on the map of Kazan and change it yourself there. For example: 65 Vosstaniya str., Kazan, Rep. Tatarstan, 420095, or call the insurance company's hotline.

### Do I need to pass a medical examination again?

If you're a 2nd, 3rd or 4th year student and your medical group is **Special 2**, then **yes**. If you are in the general or preparation group, then no.
