🐱 **catix** is an assistant for students, teachers and everyone interested in information about Innopolis. Instead of digging through chats, documents and websites, just ask a question in plain words — catix finds the answer and shows where it came from.

Here is what it can do:

- 📚 Reads university documents, Innopolis websites and study chats
- 📅 Schedule, exams, deadlines and student tips
- 🏫 Clubs, events, rooms and the campus map
- 🗓️ Add events to your calendar, book a room or sign up for an event — after your confirmation
- 📎 Send a picture or a file, and catix will read it

Your questions stay in your chat history, and nothing is changed without your approval 💜
